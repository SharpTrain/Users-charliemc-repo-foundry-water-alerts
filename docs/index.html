Water Alert SMS Program

The Foundry at Washington Park — Newburgh, NY

The Foundry at Washington Park Condominium Association monitors building-wide water usage via BlueBot and sends automated SMS alerts when abnormal consumption is detected — helping residents catch leaks early. Use the form below to request alerts.

Request Water Alert SMS

What Messages Look Like

You will only receive a message when a water spike is detected in your building phase:

[Foundry WP] Water spike in Phase 1: +47% above normal. Possible leak. Check for running toilets or open valves. Reply STOP to opt out.
[Foundry WP] PERSISTENT Water spike in Phase 2: 185 gal/hr (+52% above avg) for 3+ hrs. Check for leaks. Reply STOP to opt out.
Message & data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How to Opt Out

Reply STOP to any alert to unsubscribe immediately. You will receive one confirmation message and no further alerts. To re-enroll, use the form above or contact the board.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or contact the board at foundrynewburgh@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

  • Mobile numbers collected are used only to deliver water usage alerts to residents of The Foundry at Washington Park.
  • Phone numbers are never shared with third parties, never used for marketing, and never sold.
  • Numbers are removed from the alert list upon opt-out (STOP reply) or written request to the board.
  • Consent records are retained by the board of directors.

Terms of Service

  • This program is operated by The Foundry at Washington Park Condominium Association.
  • Alerts are automated and event-triggered — not sent on a fixed schedule.
  • The association is not liable for delayed or undelivered messages due to carrier issues.
  • Participation is voluntary. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP.
