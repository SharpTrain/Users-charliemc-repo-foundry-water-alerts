Water Alert SMS Program

The Foundry at Washington Park — Newburgh, NY

The Foundry at Washington Park Condominium Association operates an automated water usage monitoring system (BlueBot) that detects abnormal water consumption that may indicate leaks or plumbing issues. This page describes how residents opt in to receive SMS alerts from this system.

How Residents Opt In

Opt-in is collected offline through a written consent process managed by the condo board of directors:

  • Residents are informed of the SMS alert program via board communications, building notices, or at annual meetings.
  • Residents who wish to receive alerts provide their mobile phone number to the board in writing — either via a paper consent form or by email to the board address (foundrynewburgh@gmail.com).
  • Consent forms and email records are retained by the board of directors.
  • No resident is added to the SMS list without their explicit written request.

What Messages Are Sent

  • Automated water spike alerts when usage exceeds 30% above normal levels for a phase of the building.
  • Persistent spike follow-up alerts if elevated usage continues for 3 or more consecutive hours.
  • Messages are sent only when a spike event is detected — this is not a marketing or promotional program.
  • Typical frequency: 0–3 messages per week, depending on building conditions.
Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How to Opt Out

Recipients can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any alert message. After opting out, no further messages will be sent. To opt back in, contact the board directly.

Help

Reply HELP to any message or contact the board at foundrynewburgh@gmail.com for assistance.

Privacy

Mobile phone numbers collected for this program are used solely to deliver water spike alerts to residents of The Foundry at Washington Park. Phone numbers are not shared with third parties, not used for marketing, and not sold. Numbers are stored securely and removed upon opt-out request.
