The board does not currently hold these app credentials. Contact Nick to obtain the Hik-Connect account login, or reconfigure the DDNS and port-forwarding to a board-owned account.

5
iVMS-4200 for on-site unified view Install iVMS-4200 (free, Windows/Mac) on one PC at the building and add all three recorders. Single dashboard for all cameras across all phases — live view, playback, export. Works alongside Hik-Connect mobile. No subscription.

Low Cost — Under $100

6
Verify Phase 3 storage first HDD LED was dim at inspection. Check Maintenance > HDD before anything else. With 30+ cameras, a 4TB drive fills in ~2–3 weeks. A Seagate SkyHawk 4TB (~$70) or 8TB (~$130) drops in without any configuration. A full drive means footage is already being overwritten.
7
Bench-test Phase 1 dead cameras first Two spare black mini dome cameras are on the Phase 2 desk, new in bags. Pull one dead BNC cable from the DVR rear and connect a spare camera directly. If the DVR shows a picture, the cable run is good and only the camera head is dead. If no picture, the problem is in the cable or the DVR channel itself. Start with the channels physically closest to the DVR room first.
